Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions our customers ask most — before placing an order.

Our standard MOQ is 100 pieces per model. For first-time orders or market testing, we are happy to discuss smaller trial orders — contact us and we will try our best to support you.
For stock or standard models: 7–15 days after order confirmation and deposit. For custom OEM/ODM orders (new mold, custom color/logo): 25–45 days depending on the customization scope. Exact time is confirmed in our quotation.
We accept T/T (bank transfer), and Alibaba Trade Assurance. Typically 30% deposit to start production, 70% balance before shipment. PayPal is available for samples.
All prices on our website are EXW (Ex-Works) factory prices, excluding tax and freight. We can quote FOB, CIF, DDP or your preferred trade term — just tell us your destination port and quantity.
Our products comply with CE, FCC, ROHS standards and we can provide certificates for your market. Custom certifications (UL, KC, PSE, etc.) can be arranged for bulk orders — please ask for details.
Yes! We are a factory with our own R&D and mold-making team. We support: custom logo printing, color, packaging, instruction manuals, and full ODM development (new designs from your drawings or ideas). One-stop from mold to finished goods.
Yes. Samples are available — sample cost is charged and refundable against a bulk order. Sample shipping takes about 3–7 days by international express (DHL/FedEx/UPS).
We ship by sea freight (economical for bulk), air freight, or international express (samples / small orders). We can arrange shipping directly to your port or door (FOB / CIF / DDP).
Every unit is 100% function-tested before packing. We provide a 12-month quality warranty on all products — defective units are replaced or credited after inspection.
Of course! You are always welcome to visit our factory in Dongguan, Guangdong, China. We can arrange airport pickup and a full factory tour. Contact us to schedule your visit.
